# The NGNS Vision: Building On-Chain Stability for Naira Liquidity

In an economic landscape defined by high inflation, currency devaluation, and restrictive financial rails, accessing reliable liquidity and protecting value isn’t just an advantage—it is a necessity. Standard traditional avenues and legacy off-ramps often come burdened with high friction, expensive transaction fees, counterparty risk, and unpredictable delays.

**NGNS** was designed to solve these systemic challenges directly on-chain. Built as a decentralized Naira-pegged stablecoin (DSC) mechanism tailored for Naira ecosystem users, NGNS serves as the financial engine within the broader **Salva** protocol on Base. It bridges the gap between local asset exposure and global decentralized finance, offering a transparent, permissionless, and hyper-efficient store of value.

---

### Key Pillars of the NGNS Ecosystem

* **Direct Capital Preservation:** Protects wealth against localized currency volatility by allowing users to lock up approved collateral assets and mint a stable, predictable, Naira-pegged unit of account.
* **True Decentralization:** Operates entirely through self-executing smart contracts without relying on centralized banks, intermediaries, or custodial gatekeepers who can freeze funds or block transactions.
* **Zero-Fee Peer-to-Peer Mobility:** Works natively alongside Salva's zero-fee P2P exchange engine and custom name registry, turning complex crypto transactions into simple, cost-effective peer-to-peer payments.
* **Capital Efficiency by Design:** Engineered at the core smart-contract level to minimize network execution costs (gas), ensuring that everyday transfers and liquidity minting remain accessible for all account sizes.
* **Empowering Financial Sovereignty:** Gives individuals and merchants total ownership over their financial transactions, enabling instant settlements and transparent risk management backed by verifiable on-chain collateral.

---

*By combining robust collateralization models with ultra-low-cost execution on Base, NGNS delivers a resilient, user-owned financial tool built specifically to withstand real-world economic volatility while keeping capital moving freely.*
